Which areas do you serve?

Ortaca, Dalaman, Köyceğiz and all their neighbourhoods, including Dalyan.

How quickly can you come?

We aim to reach Dalyan and Ortaca within 1 hour, Dalaman and Köyceğiz within 2 hours. We work 24/7.

The power is out. Should I call you?

If the outage covers your street or neighbourhood, call the distribution company's fault line (186). If only your home or business has no power, call us.

Are you an authorised service centre?

No. Gün Technic is an independent technical service and works on air conditioners of every brand. For units still under warranty or brand-new products, please contact the brand directly.

Do you also do cameras, alarms and installation work?

Yes. From security cameras and alarms to curtain rails, TV wall mounts, light fittings, furniture and water heaters, we do every job that needs drilling, fixing and connecting.

Is the site survey free?

Yes, the survey is free. We look on site and tell you a clear price.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. Our workmanship has a 15-day warranty; materials are covered by the manufacturer's own warranty.
